# Privacy Policy — BUKONZO SACCO Mobile App

**Draft for review by Bukonzo United Teachers Cooperative Society and its legal
adviser before publication.** This must be hosted at a public URL (e.g.
`https://www.bukonzounitedteacherscooperativesociety.com/privacy`) and that URL
entered in the Google Play Data Safety form and the App Store privacy
questionnaire. The app links to it from **Profile → Privacy Policy**.

_Last updated: [DATE]_

## 1. Who we are

The BUKONZO SACCO mobile app is operated by Bukonzo United Teachers Cooperative
Society ("the SACCO", "we", "us"). Contact: [support email], [phone], [postal
address].

## 2. Information we collect

**You provide directly (during registration / profile completion):**
- Full name, date of birth, gender, marital status
- National Identification Number (NIN) and other KYC identifiers
- Phone number and email address
- Residential address
- Next-of-kin name, relationship and phone number
- Employment / income details (when applying for a loan)
- Profile photo (optional)

**Generated by your use of the app:**
- Account balances, savings, shares and loan records
- Transaction history (deposits, withdrawals, repayments), including the mobile
  money number used and the provider reference for each transaction
- In-app notifications

**Collected automatically:**
- Authentication session data (a secure login cookie stored on your device)
- Basic device information required for the app to function

The app does **not** collect location data, contacts, or advertising
identifiers.

## 3. How we use your information

- To create and administer your SACCO membership and accounts
- To process deposits, withdrawals and loan repayments via MTN Mobile Money and
  Relworx
- To meet legal, regulatory and anti-money-laundering (KYC) obligations
- To communicate with you about your account and transactions
- To provide customer support

## 4. Who we share it with

- **Payment providers** — MTN Mobile Money and Relworx — to execute the
  transactions you request. They receive the mobile money number and amount.
- **Our technology providers** — hosting and file-storage services used to run
  the SACCO's systems, bound by confidentiality obligations.
- **Regulators and authorities** — where required by Ugandan law.

We do **not** sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

## 5. Data retention

We retain your personal and financial records for as long as you are a member
and thereafter for the period required by cooperative, tax and
anti-money-laundering law.

## 6. Security

Login sessions are protected with encrypted cookies and per-request CSRF tokens.
Transaction requests are authenticated and validated server-side. You can enable
biometric (fingerprint / Face ID) lock on your device in **Profile → Security**.

## 7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data,
subject to the record-keeping obligations above. To do so, contact us using the
details in Section 1. Deleting your data may require closing your membership.

## 8. Children

The app is for SACCO members and staff and is not intended for anyone under 18.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes at this URL and update the "Last updated" date.
